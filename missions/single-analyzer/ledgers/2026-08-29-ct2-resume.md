## Corrections/false starts worth remembering

- Don't infer a blanket "writes to worktree X are blocked" from one blocked call (`Edit`
  on `.git/info/exclude`) — the actual restriction is specifically about paths outside
  any worktree root, not about cross-worktree writes to `session-tracking` content in
  general (which the settings-level permission does grant, but the `EnterWorktree`
  pinning mechanism still vetoes structurally regardless of that grant). Test the
  specific tool/path in question, not a generalization from an adjacent failure.
- When a coder agent's isolated worktree looks like it started from the wrong base,
  don't assume it's the coder's mistake or something fixable by editing the *target*
  worktree (`single-analyzer` here had nothing uncommitted) — the mismatch is in how the
  isolated worktree itself got created, a separate thing to root-cause before retrying.
